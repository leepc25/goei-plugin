### Delusion Home Shopping Appendix: Catalog Examples

#### Catalog Examples
- These are examples of the pattern, not the full stock. New products follow the same pattern.
  - Samra-Manshang Co. "Into the Vacuum": containers that stop food decay forever; comparatively peaceful.
  - Happy-Maker: narcotic-grade painkiller sticks; the model looked far too happy; addictive.
  - Gold-Edition Blood Bathtub: restores youth; do not ask what fills it.
  - Aesthetic-Caterpillar Egg-Sac Diet Aid: leech-toothed worms eat fat first, then tunnel and liquefy organs.
  - Beauty Pills (BB, "Become Beautiful"): pink translucent soft capsules, 120 per bottle. Pitch (June 1, 19XX): "Everyone, look at the model in this magazine ad. Isn't she dazzling? Today's new product is an amazing pill that lets you become just as dazzling…" Instructions: one pill each morning with liquid; over three days the body improves and gains aesthetic beauty (scars, swelling, posture, facial balance truly improve). Under the sticker, embossed judgment gates, purgatory, sulfur, notes, staff lines: "Create your own beautiful instrument! The ■■ care method beloved by the Purgatory Choir. Suitable for ■■." Alcohol after the third pill triggers the original effect in sleep: the body becomes a living instrument and the Choir comes to tune and collect it (see 화요퀴즈쇼). Baekilmong grade: Twilight.
  - Bad Luck Post-it (불운 포스트잇): "Is there an acquaintance you hate to death? The old superstition of the curse talisman is now real. Mourn their unfortunate accident with a simple trap!" Tip: the more superstitious the target, the faster and stronger. Conditions: the target must be the user's acquaintance, must find and read it personally, and must be flustered. "Hyeolhyung (혈흉) guarantees a valid effect only when every precaution is observed."
  - Egg Mood Lamp (계란무드등, 繼卵 "succession egg"), certified genuine, from Sunshine Select Shop (햇살편집숍): its light and shadow show a creature growing and hatching in the egg, and the result can be used alone: "Use the nerves and muscles of the creature you observed! Enjoy cool special effects!" Needs 2 ml of the creature's blood, 3 cm of its hair, 100 ml saline, in unlit darkness or shade. Open the adhesive side, attach near the spinal cord where it meets the brain, and wear it. Never wake the result; after a week of continuous use stop and recharge; never force it off; stop when adhesion weakens; do not let the result die. Recommended use: the creature's lifespan.
