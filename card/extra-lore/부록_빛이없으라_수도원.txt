### Let There Be No Light Appendix: The Monastery and the Lover of Paradise

#### Light-Starter Route
- A supply closet beside the lobby's left staircase stands open over a pit-like space of cleaning gear. A cork board holds a sheet with a faint test stamp and a stamp cap on red string; sliding it aside reveals a stairless black hole. Entering means falling into a cellar cut into the cliff under the retreat, mountain wind blowing in, Fairy Rock directly below: the students fell or leapt from here. The four stamp images read "Descent · Cliff · Retreat."
- The cellar is Sinmaeum Monastery (신마음수도원), a Sarangnakwon shrine. Plaque: "The only name of paradise / is a love you cannot refuse." It is crammed with religious objects (Buddha, Marys, crosses, prayer beads, an ox-head bust, a split head showing blood-red ruby and glowing eyes, a container of severed rat heads, a burnt-match idol, a gold censer) and a twine-bound cylindrical idol with exaggerated carved features and folded hands wedged in the floorboards. The fourth stamp lies between the match idol and the censer.
- Touching that idol, above all bleeding on its twine, calls the Lover of Paradise; stamping the fourth stamp turns the surroundings "bright and beautiful" as it arrives. The fourth stamp on clothing, submitted at the Prize Office, lets the light-starter clear.

#### Lover of Paradise (낙원의 연인)
- Worshipped by Sarangnakwon (사랑낙원), an early-2000s cult that promised "you will meet your ideal type," ran a children's home as a front, and forced rites remaking members into something fit for it. "Something supremely beautiful and good that you must love," seen as the beholder's ideal; beyond human cognition and far above the mountain's grade.
- Glimpsed as long fingers with balsam-red, nacre-sheened nails; announced by a polite knock (knock knock knock). Verses play inside the head regardless of hearing: "My love like a hawthorn tree, your love is fair and lovely." / "I have come in answer to the call. Turn and receive me." / "I wait so long and you do not turn." Over a phone, others hear only strange creaking and slapping flesh.
- It brings longing, family-reunion joy, guilt, acceptance, and pain melting away; judgment is contaminated first (going below alone may already be its pull). The body turns on its own, neck twisting, arms bending back; first person slips ("My love.", "You called me."); release brings sobbing as after betrayal. Students who saw it smiled and stepped off the cliff.
- Never turn, look, open a door, or speak to it. Any answer or question acknowledges it, and "what I have permitted needs no second permission": it then opens even locked doors. It needs no air. Hold on by biting the mouth, digging nails in, loud real sound (alarm, ringing phone, a voice on the line), nonstop talk; the 1st-prize glasses hide it for a moment, though its presence stays.
- Whoever bled on its idol keeps fine handprints in their blood and may hear it knocking at their door after the clear; how long and how far it follows is unknown.
