===== STREET ENTRANCE (jr_entry, 7 panels, day) =====
The 6 attached images are STYLE reference only (pixel look, dark outlines, shading, colors). Do not copy their objects, layouts or composition.

Draw 7 panels, ① to ⑦. Side by side they form one continuous street in Jungnim-dong, Seoul: a row of run-down 3-story commercial buildings about to be demolished, with the entrance to "Jungnim Underground Arcade" at the right end. Daytime, overcast. Each panel is a separate image.

MUST
1. Draw every panel by hand. No code, no copy-paste, cropping, tiling, mirroring, resizing or color reduction.
2. PIXEL GRID: each panel is pixel art on a 632×424 grid, output at 1536×1024, so one grid pixel = one ~2.4×2.4 px square block. Outlines are 1 block thick. No detail smaller than 1 block, including sign letters. No anti-aliasing, gradients or blur.
3. COLORS: at most 64 colors per panel, the same palette in all 7 panels (faded beige, grey and rust base + a few bright accents: sign colors, green kiosk).
4. SIZES in grid pixels (a person is 150 tall): shop door 170×80; shop window bottom 40 (shin); vending machine 160 tall; box stack 45 (knee); trash bag 35; potted plant 30; bottle or can 8–10.
5. CAMERA: standing eye height, looking ~20° down. Building base line horizontal at y=254 (60%) in every panel. Sidewalk y=254–320, curb at y=320, asphalt road y=330–424. The frame shows the ground floor and the bottom of the 2nd floor; the sky is not visible.
6. One shop per panel; its front fills ~80% of the panel width. Shop widths, heights and sign sizes differ.
7. DEPTH: shop entrances are recessed into the facade. Awnings, projecting signs and AC units stick out and cast shadows on the sidewalk. Props stand on the sidewalk in front of the buildings: bottom edge clearly below y=254, top and side faces visible, dark shadow.
8. SEAMS: left and right 10% of every panel are identical in design — a plain concrete wall strip between buildings with one vertical drainpipe, sidewalk, curb and road only; same colors, brightness and line heights in all 7 panels.
9. DECAY AND MESS: half-lowered rusty shutters, marks where old signs were torn off, X-shaped tape on cracked windows, peeling paint, rust streaks, tangled power lines. Litter scattered over the whole sidewalk and the road edge (crumpled paper, crushed cans, cigarette butts, plastic scraps, flattened cardboard, leaves), puddles, cracked and patched paving, weeds in cracks. Trash-bag piles at the building bases. Every piece a different shape, size, color and angle.
10. SIGNS: exact Korean name in quotes, large, chunky pixel lettering with depth (raised letters with shadow, chipped paint, or faded paint on metal). Each sign a different form: ① long board above an awning, ② long board with a cross, ③ green long board, ④ board above a yellow awning, ⑤ light box, ⑥ raised letters on a rusty plate, ⑦ sign on the kiosk roof.
11. ENDS: no side walls. The street continues out of the frame at both ends.

NEVER
- Repeated or duplicated elements: the same litter, stain, box, window, shutter, floor patch or wall detail twice
- Clean, tidy or new-looking buildings
- Objects flat against the wall; small items bigger than the sizes above
- Fine detail smaller than one grid pixel; smooth gradients; more than 64 colors
- A shop, sign or object in the seam strips
- One vanishing point for the whole street; slanted building fronts; sidewalk tile grid converging in perspective
- Large objects in the middle of the sidewalk or in front of the arcade entrance
- Smooth computer-font letters; any text other than the given names and the bus numbers; text on flyers, posters or banners
- People; cars; chairs, stools, benches

PANELS (one shop each)
① Bus stop shelter on the sidewalk (no bench) with a route sign "271 606 701 702", and laundry "명진세탁" with a green awning and clothes hanging inside.
② Pharmacy "충정약국": glass front with medicine shelves, a red cross on the sign.
③ Real estate office "성진부동산": glass front with listing boards (pictures of buildings only, no text); a torn blank redevelopment banner hanging on the wall.
④ Side-dish shop "미도반찬": yellow awning, display of side dishes in trays, stacked plastic crates out front.
⑤ Stationery shop "은성문구": glass front with stacked paper and supplies; a vending machine on the sidewalk beside it.
⑥ Electrical shop "만리전기": lamps, cables and parts inside; cable spools and a hand truck out front; a utility pole with tangled wires.
⑦ Right end: the arcade entrance — a green steel kiosk with a roof standing on the sidewalk, sign "중림지하상가", open on its left side, a stairway inside going down out of sight, waist-high rails, warm yellow light rising from below. Clear in front of the opening.

B2 is the old storage level: bare concrete, exposed pipes, warehouse units turned into shady shops, darker and colder than B1. A few dim fluorescent tubes (some dead), small caged wall lamps. Each panel is a separate image.

4. SIZES in grid pixels (a person is 150 tall): shop entrance 220 tall; steel door 170×80; counter / display case 75 tall (waist); equipment case or box 45 (knee); trash bag 35; prosthetic arm 35; bottle or jar 8–10; key 2–3.
