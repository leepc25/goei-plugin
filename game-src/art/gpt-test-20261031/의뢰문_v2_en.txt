===== FRONT (B1, 7 panels) =====
The 6 attached images are STYLE reference only (pixel look, dark outlines, shading, colors). Do not copy their objects, layouts or composition.

Draw 7 panels, ① to ⑦. Side by side they form one continuous corridor: B1 of "Jungnim Underground Arcade", a run-down 1990s Seoul underground mall that is secretly a black market. Each panel is a separate image, drawn in order.

MUST
1. Draw every panel by hand. No code, no copy-paste, cropping, tiling, mirroring, resizing or color reduction.
2. PIXEL GRID: each panel is pixel art on a 632×424 grid, output at 1536×1024, so one grid pixel = one ~2.4×2.4 px square block. Outlines are 1 block thick. No detail smaller than 1 block, including sign letters. No anti-aliasing, gradients or blur.
3. COLORS: at most 64 colors per panel, the same palette in all 7 panels (dark, desaturated base + a few bright accents: warm shop light, neon).
4. SIZES in grid pixels (a person is 150 tall): shop entrance 220 tall; door 170×80; counter / display table 75 tall (waist); box stack 45 (knee); trash bag 35; bottle or can 8–10; hand tool 10–15; key 2–3.
5. CAMERA: standing eye height, looking ~20° down. Back-wall base line horizontal at y=254 (60%) in every panel. Floor = bottom 170 px (40%). Ceiling = thin top strip.
6. One shop per panel; its front fills ~80% of the panel width.
7. DEPTH: each shop is a recessed room — its inner side walls and ceiling visible going inward. Pillars stick out from the wall with visible side faces. Counters, carts and crates stand on the floor in front of the wall: bottom edge clearly below y=254, top and side faces visible, dark floor shadow.
8. CONTINUOUS PANELS: draw ① first, then draw each next panel as the direct continuation of the previous one. The left 10% of each panel from ② on shows exactly what is in the right 10% of the previous panel (same pillar, same wall stains, same floor stains and litter, same lines), so neighboring panels overlap and join. Every line cut at a panel's right edge (ceiling, lights, pipes, wall base, floor edge) continues at the same height in the next panel. The edge 10% of each panel contains only one concrete pillar, wall, floor and ceiling; no shop, sign or large object.
9. FLOOR: one seamless slab of worn concrete — NO tiles, NO grid, NO joint lines or seams anywhere on the floor, because grid lines break when panels are joined. Litter scattered over the whole walkway, not only at the walls (crumpled paper, crushed cans, cigarette butts, bottle caps, plastic scraps, flattened cardboard), plus puddles and oil stains. Garbage piles at wall bases (collapsed boxes, burst trash bags, bottles). Every piece a different shape, size, color and angle.
10. SIGNS: exact Korean name in quotes, large. The letters are drawn with the same chunky pixel blocks as the rest of the image, with stair-stepped edges — NOT a computer font. Letters have depth (raised letters with shadow, chipped paint, or glowing neon). Each sign a different form: ① long board, ② vertical blade sign on a pillar, ③ text on a torn awning, ④ paint on glass, ⑥ round sign, ⑦ green-cross light box.
11. ENDS: side walls only at the two ends. ① left edge: left wall seen angled inward. ⑦ right edge: right wall seen angled inward. No side walls between panels.

NEVER
- Repeated or duplicated elements: the same litter, stain, box, shelf, floor patch or wall detail twice
- Clean or tidy look
- Objects flat against the wall; small items bigger than the sizes above
- Fine detail smaller than one grid pixel; smooth gradients; more than 64 colors
- A shop, sign or large object in the edge 10%; bright light pools at the edges
- Floor tiles, grid or joint lines; one vanishing point for the whole corridor; slanted back wall
- Large objects in the middle of the walkway or in front of doors, passages or stairs
- Stairs or fire doors on the back wall; stairs seen head-on
- Computer-font or smooth vector letters on signs; any text other than the given names; text on flyers; walls covered in paper
- People (even inside shops); chairs, stools, benches

PANELS (one shop each)
① Left end: stairs up along the left wall, ~1/5 of floor width, rising from the foreground toward the back and up out of the top-left, metal rails. Open snack bar "은정분식": counter sticking out onto the walkway, fish-cake pot, tteokbokki griddle, standing counter only.
② Open hardware store "성원철물": tiny tools piled everywhere; wheeled cart out on the walkway.
③ Closed tailor "동진수선": rusty dented shutter with graffiti; loaded hand truck in front.
④ "대박 알선": tinted glass front and glass door, red neon "OPEN" reflected in a floor puddle; clear in front of the door.
⑤ Narrow dark corridor cut into the back wall to the back alley, up-arrow sign; clear in front.
⑥ Open general store "금성생활": small fans, heaters and junk overflowing onto the walkway.
⑦ Clinic "손길의원": cloudy glass door; clear in front of the door. Right end: rectangular stairwell cut into the floor along the right wall, ~1/5 of floor width, going down toward the back into darkness, waist-high rail on its walkway edge, "B2 ↓" plate on the right wall.

Output: 7 landscape images (3:2), ① first.
