A three-seat waiting chair from the waiting area of a Korean bank: three upholstered seats with padded fabric cushions and padded backrests, joined side by side on one metal frame, with armrests between the seats and at both ends. Seen from directly behind: the covered backs of the three backrests face the viewer, and the seat cushions are hidden behind them. Seen almost level, from the eye height of a standing adult, looking only a little down. The chair is much wider than it is tall and fills almost the whole width of the canvas. Only the chair, centered, nothing else.
