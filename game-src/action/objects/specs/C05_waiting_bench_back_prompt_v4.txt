A three-seat waiting chair from the waiting area of a Korean bank: three seats with padded black faux leather cushions and padded black backrests, joined side by side on one dark metal frame that stands on slim metal legs, with the legs and the open space under the seats clearly visible; not a sofa, no armrests and no holes anywhere. Seen from directly behind: the plain black backs of the three backrests face the viewer, and the seat cushions are hidden behind them. Seen almost level, from the eye height of a standing adult, looking only a little down. The chair is much wider than it is tall and fills almost the whole width of the canvas. Only the chair, centered, nothing else.
