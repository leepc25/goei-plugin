A waiting room bench of three connected seats, like in a Korean bank: three molded seats in one row, joined on one long metal beam with metal legs. The bench is seen from its back side: the backs of the three backrests face the viewer. Seen almost straight from the front, from the eye height of a standing adult, looking only a little down. The bench is much wider than it is tall and fills almost the whole width of the canvas. Only the bench, centered, nothing else.
