A waiting room bench of three connected seats, like in a Korean bank: three molded seats in one row, joined on one long metal beam with metal legs. The bench is seen from directly behind: only the smooth plain rear shells of the three backrests face the viewer, and the seat surfaces are not visible at all. Seen almost straight from the front, from the eye height of a standing adult, looking only a little down. The bench is much wider than it is tall and fills almost the whole width of the canvas. Only the bench, centered, nothing else.
