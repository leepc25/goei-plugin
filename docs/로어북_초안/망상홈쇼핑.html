Delusion Home Shopping (망상홈쇼핑)

Identity

  • A 24-hour 2000s-style TV shopping channel selling anomalous merchandise whose advertised effects are real. It is a platform, not a maker: it sources items from other Uh-doom and workshops and markets them for whatever demand exists, often for a purpose other than the original.
  • Once a Friday-night ad that cut into ordinary TV; now the tenant of Blood Broadcasting Station at Segwang's Afternoon Station (see 오후역). Its broadcasts, app, and goods reach everywhere.

Contact

  • Friday Interruption: screens left on switch to the channel; changing channels brings it back.
  • Live App: appears on phones uninvited and makes the owner an audience member. Its chat mixes shopping talk with pleas from people trapped inside.
  • Stray Goods: boxes with the channel's sticker turn up in pharmacies, gyms, flea markets, stockrooms.

Studio Audience

  • A banner ("Studio audience wanted! Free seats, special audience prices") appears on air and in the app. Applicants are pulled into the studio seats at the next broadcast; anyone touching them comes as their guest.
  • Audience members get a numbered paddle and pass. This guest status protects them while kept. Rules: applaud on cue; never cross the floor tape; never volunteer when the host asks "Who would like to try it?" (volunteers become demonstration models); touch nothing on stage; stay seated until the credits roll and the EXIT sign lights, then leave by that door, which returns them to where they applied.
  • During breaks a runner shows a glass-cased showroom. Samples may be examined; opening a case alone counts as buying. Prices can be haggled with runners or with Red Friday on air.
  • Payment is either "interest-free installments," each one an obligation to appear later as a demonstration model, or a trade in anomalous items, Uh-doom material, or contracts. The channel underpays for anything it understands better than the seller. Taking the gift bag at the exit finalizes the sale.
  • Phone orders during broadcasts use the same installments and arrive by courier, often addressed to someone else; opening the box accepts it. Missed installments mean being summoned onto the set without warning. No refunds.

Merchandise

  • The catalog is open-ended. Every product has a cheerful ad claim, a real effect, strict usage instructions, and a hidden original purpose visible under the sticker or in embossed marks. Following the instructions exactly gives only the advertised effect; breaking them releases the original purpose. Examples:
  • Into the Vacuum: containers that stop food decay forever.
  • Happy-Maker: narcotic-grade painkiller sticks; addictive.
  • Gold-Edition Blood Bathtub: restores youth; do not ask what fills it.
  • Aesthetic-Caterpillar Egg-Sac Diet Aid: leech-toothed worms eat fat, then organs.
  • Beauty Pills (BB): one pink capsule each morning with liquid; over three days the body grows beautiful. Hidden purpose: the Purgatory Choir's instrument-care method. Alcohol after the third pill turns the sleeper into a living instrument (see 화요퀴즈쇼).
  • Bad Luck Post-it: a curse note for a hated acquaintance; the more superstitious, the stronger. Works only if the target is an acquaintance, finds and reads it personally, and panics. The maker, Hyeolhyung (혈흉), guarantees nothing otherwise.
  • Egg Mood Lamp (계란무드등, Sunshine Select Shop): cultures a creature from 2 ml of its blood, 3 cm of its hair, and 100 ml saline in darkness. The hatched result is attached near the user's spinal cord to use its nerves and muscles. Never wake it, never force it off, recharge after a week, and keep it alive.

Host

  • Red Friday: black suit, white makeup, huge fully dilated black eyes, smooth relentless sales patter; off air his mouth opens onto a bottomless scarlet throat. Other hosts and staff share the face and throat. Dead contract workers keep working. He falters only when the landlord's rent call cuts in, and broadcasts grow harsher as rent day nears.
