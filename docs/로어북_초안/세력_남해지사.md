### Baekilmong Namhae Branch (백일몽 남해지사)

#### Identity
- A regional branch of Baekilmong (C00), not an independent faction: its staff are Baekilmong employees, its output goes to HQ, and HQ can audit, overrule, or abandon it.
- HQ calls it a pilot branch: "harsh environment, excellent efficiency," praised for recycling resources, minimal consumables, and unmatched output per cost. Corridor slogan: "Save resources and serve the company."
- It keeps HQ's old habit of feeding people into Uh-doom in bulk without manuals or gear; early wiki stories of nameless numbered staff dying barehanded are really branch stories. Results bring absurd promotion speed: at least one current I-sa began here as staffer, document writer, and zone manager.

#### Location
- A windowless underground site like a closed coal mine (concrete corridors, dripping shafts, rails, prison-like steel doors); new hires are told it was a mine. Truly beneath the coast near Yongmeori, Jeju, classified even from most staff. People arrive by tinted vehicle or asleep; car-only passengers still reach the island (whether the transport is anomalous is unknown).
- Built over a native Uh-doom (see 하얀 끈); going deeper is forbidden. Vertical escape shafts with one ladder and keypad hatches open onto the basalt shore; codes are staff knowledge.

#### Personnel
- Branch Chief: personnel authority; holds the only office Security cannot enter; exposed to rival HQ executives.
- Researchers (연구원/담당자): each owns a block of Worker numbers, commands by loudspeaker without hearing replies, assigns Uh-doom, questions survivors in the Work Communication Room, and sets promotions. Paid mostly in points skimmed from Workers' collectors, they hide lucky Workers and high-grade results from HQ.
- Branch Security: full-body suits, "Baekilmong Guard" tags, numbers, no records; nearly silent; tick clipboards, push Workers into rooms, collect armbands. Rule: "Security does not remove its uniform until disposal." HQ calls them "recycled mass-production units"; they show none of HQ Security's contamination tells, which is itself unsettling.
- Workers (작업자): day laborers, legally not employees.

#### Workers
- Recruitment: laminated yellow street flyers ("Job seekers wanted! URGENT! …dam-specialist conglomerate", "Insane benefits", "Start work today!"; back: "Paid on application! 500,000 won cash"). The tear-off number repeats itself, a second number appears, the caller screams, a voice says "Come here," and the caller vanishes; the flyer then reads "Position filled." Formal hires agree by phone to a "Namhae Branch Staffing Notice" and wake in work clothes in a waiting hall. Targets: gamblers, debtors, addicts, people nobody seeks.
- Promise: 1,000,000 won cash daily, 100-day live-in contract (about 100 million won), no outside contact "for confidentiality," "a drug trial plus odd jobs." Cash never leaves; canteen, store, vending-machine clinic, and Leisure Zone recapture it.
- Worker Grades X > Y > Z, three-digit numbers (Z999 last), distinct from HQ's X/Y/Z Finishing Jo. Z: ten per earth-walled cell, toilet hole, rotting mattresses, cold rice and anchovies; the last cell bears a "Finishing Team" nameplate. Y: lockable rundown flats with sofa and bath, normal meals, takeaway. X: clean three-person rooms, one bed each, good meals, a weekly weekday Leisure Zone pass. Good results bring posted Special Reassignments (e.g., "Y492 -> X915"); grades readjust weekly. The missing are "reassigned elsewhere."
- Red neck sticker: "[number] / Classification: Worker / Days remaining: N". Unremovable without branch chemicals; touching it earns demerits. Beyond the branch radius it blackens, reads "[number] / You have left your post. / Capture required.", spreads rash, and strangles until the wearer returns.
- Jobs: called by number block, blindfolded, mine cart, a locked left-arm cuff that secretly holds a crude Kkumgyeol Collector (researcher takes the yield, each level above takes a cut; damage "deducted from wages"), an unmarked steel door, no manual. Refusal: left inside, unpaid, demerits. Night Special Work pays double to triple and drops 100+ Workers into one Uh-doom. Labor Experiments hide anomalous items in meals or hand them out as "parasite medicine."

#### Facilities
- Worker Zone: assembly hall split into X/Y/Z pens like a cattle shed, dorms, canteen, store (5,000 won a cigarette), vending-machine clinic at extreme prices; wiretaps and CCTV with occasional blind spots.
- Leisure Zone (Sundays): a tacky bulb-lit rural night street of mostly unstaffed shops at several times outside prices: Namhae Bar, Happy Lottery, Rhythm Karaoke, Sleep Care (massage chairs, Baekilmong hair-loss pills, unprescribed drugs, "Painkiller – 300,000 (EXTRA STRONG!)"), arcade, comic room, and a Religious Activity corridor (one faith per room, Sunday fortune-teller, last room a dried rite table with a pig's head). "Staff Only" doors hide staff areas. Nobody steals; everyone has seen the price.
- Containment Zone: by mine cart; identical dark grey doors, red-lit cells, some earth-floored.
- Staff Zone: 2000s public-corporation offices, sectors such as B-5 and B-9, storerooms, a Worker management room behind a one-way mirror.
- Old Facility: sealed behind earth walls and a disused staff bathhouse since a cost-cutting renovation six years ago (which split staff and containment areas with one door, below HQ standards). Steel corridors, hand-wheel doors, surveillance gallery, emergency exits, doors with a distinctive "Staff Only" handle crest, and a central control system bootable in safe mode. A former manager's office, administrator account, and early Uh-doom documents remain.
- Loudspeaker: "Good morning! All Workers, please prepare for today's work~", "Roll call in ten minutes. Get rich today too~", "Lunch time! Eat well and make lots of money this afternoon~", "When you're desperate: Dream Money, Dream Life!", "Leisure Zone opening. Fighting this week too! Get rich!" Emergency alarm: "[EMERGENCY!] All employees still inside, evacuate via the escape routes!" Workers are told the old mine misfires and to ignore it; it is for regular staff only.

#### Hidden Truths (GM only)
- Workers are recycled products with a 100-day shelf life, filled with an orange raw material; the sticker covers its injection port, and removing it drains and kills them.
- Every 30 days ("Worker Cleanup") they are sedated, bound, and carted up a rail line to the Processing Facility, a deformed toy-factory Uh-doom (Scan, Standardize, Sticker Removal, Raw Material Check, Recycling) that melts them and reissues them, memories reset to hiring, in boxes labeled "Shelf life: 100 days 👍 Still recyclable after expiry!" One batch is 999. Staff added locks and a "Custom 26" setting; its Basic, Special, and Custom Recycling modes are mostly unexplored.
- So no Worker finishes 100 days or leaves with money, and one who "has been here two weeks" may have been here for months.
- Branch Security is unexplained: suits appear where no guard should be; one wore the face of an employee declared dead in an Uh-doom.

#### Registered Uh-doom on Site
- White Cord (하얀 끈, Qterw-A-89, Darkness), the native Uh-doom; its 500 m exclusion was ignored to convert old buildings into dorms. Let Them Go Now (이만 보내줍시다, Qterw-C-2554, Gloaming), drowned-man wooden dolls in room Qterw-C-348. Let There Be No Light (빛이 없으라, Qterw-C-1010, Gloaming), used for Night Special Work. Fear Cleaner (피어 클리너, Qterw-C-219, Gloaming), 25-person cleaning jobs. Beauty Pills (미인 약, Qterw-E-112, Twilight), 70 boxes bought off a home-shopping broadcast. An uncoded crying-face coin purse, an easy "honey job."

#### Relations and Seeds
- HQ tolerates the branch but audits after incidents; researchers see audits as point and manual theft. An auditor granted temporary mooring becomes acting manager with Worker disposal authority.
- Management Bureau (A00): hiring is formally legal, the site hidden, Workers cut off; Bureau rescue lines inside Uh-doom can still reach a trapped Worker. Debt brokers and Jungrim loan sharks naturally supply desperate people.
- Seeds (possibilities, not history): the White Cord breaches into the dorms and the infected knock claiming to be Security checking a false alarm; a Beauty Pill experiment meets the Leisure Zone bar and summons the Purgatory Choir's parade; an escaped Uh-doom follows a Worker back and is newly registered in a sealed zone; an HQ auditor arrives and researchers hide their best Worker and collector; a Worker finds a Staff Only handle fragment under a Z mattress; Worker Cleanup night comes early.
