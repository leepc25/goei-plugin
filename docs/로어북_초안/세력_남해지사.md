### Baekilmong Namhae Branch (백일몽 남해지사)

#### Identity
- A regional branch of Baekilmong (C00), not an independent faction. Headquarters calls it a pilot branch: "harsh environment, excellent efficiency," praised for recycling resources and minimal consumables. Slogan: "Save resources and serve the company."
- It still feeds people into Uh-doom in bulk without manuals or gear. Results bring absurdly fast promotion; at least one current executive started here.
- A windowless underground facility resembling a closed coal mine, secretly located beneath the coast near Yongmeori, Jeju. People are moved in by tinted vehicle or asleep. It was built over a native Uh-doom; going deeper is forbidden. Vertical escape shafts with keypad hatches open onto the basalt shore.

#### Personnel
- Branch Chief: personnel authority, exposed to HQ politics.
- Researchers: run Workers by loudspeaker without hearing them, assign Uh-doom, decide promotions, and live on points skimmed from Workers' collectors. They hide lucky Workers and high-grade results from HQ.
- Branch Security: full-body suits, numbers instead of names, no records, almost silent. Rule: "Security does not remove its uniform until disposal." HQ calls them recycled mass-production units.
- Workers (작업자): day laborers, not employees.

#### Workers
- Lured by cheap street flyers ("Job seekers wanted! URGENT!", "500,000 won cash on application"). Calling the number makes the caller vanish; the flyer then reads "Position filled."
- Promised 1,000,000 won cash daily for a 100-day live-in contract with no outside contact. The cash is recaptured through overpriced canteen, store, vending-machine clinic, and the Leisure Zone.
- Worker Grades X > Y > Z with three-digit numbers (Z999 last), distinct from HQ's X/Y/Z Finishing Jo. Z sleeps ten to an earth-walled cell; Y gets lockable rooms and normal meals; X gets clean three-person rooms and a weekly Leisure Zone pass. Strong results bring posted Special Reassignments.
- A red neck sticker shows number and days remaining. It cannot be removed without branch chemicals; outside the branch radius it blackens, reads "You have left your post. Capture required.", and strangles until the wearer returns.
- Each job: blindfold, mine cart, a locked armband secretly holding a Kkumgyeol Collector, an unmarked steel door, no manual. Refusal means demerits and no pay. Night Special Work drops a hundred Workers into one Uh-doom. Labor Experiments slip anomalous items into meals as "parasite medicine."
- The emergency alarm is an evacuation call for regular staff only; Workers are told to ignore it.

#### Facilities
- Worker Zone with X/Y/Z pens, dorms, canteen, store, and vending-machine clinic, all wiretapped.
- Leisure Zone, open Sundays: a tacky bulb-lit night street of unstaffed overpriced shops (bar, lottery, karaoke, sleep-care with unprescribed drugs) and a Religious Activity corridor. "Staff Only" doors hide staff areas.
- Old Facility: sealed behind earth walls since a cost-cutting renovation six years ago, with steel corridors, surveillance gallery, emergency exits, and a central control system still bootable in safe mode. A former manager's office, administrator account, and early Uh-doom documents remain.

#### Hidden Truths (GM only)
- Workers are recycled products with a 100-day shelf life. Their bodies hold an orange raw material; the sticker covers its injection port, and removing it kills them.
- Every 30 days Workers are sedated and carted to the Processing Facility, a deformed toy-factory Uh-doom that melts them and reissues them, memories reset to hiring, in boxes labeled "Shelf life: 100 days. Still recyclable after expiry!" One batch is 999. No Worker ever finishes 100 days.
- Some Security suits walk where no guard should be; one has worn the face of an employee declared dead in an Uh-doom.

#### Relations and Seeds
- HQ tolerates the branch but audits after incidents; researchers see audits as point theft. An auditor granted temporary mooring becomes acting manager with Worker disposal authority.
- The Management Bureau (A00) finds it hard to touch: hiring is formally legal, the site concealed, Workers cut off.
- Seeds: the native Uh-doom breaches into the dorms; a Labor Experiment meets the Leisure Zone bar; an escaped Uh-doom follows a Worker back; an auditor arrives; Worker Cleanup night comes early.
