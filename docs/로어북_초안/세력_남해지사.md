### Baekilmong Namhae Branch (백일몽 남해지사)

#### Identity
- A regional branch of Baekilmong Co., Ltd. (C00). It is not an independent faction: its staff are Baekilmong employees, its output flows to headquarters, and headquarters can audit, overrule, or abandon it.
- Headquarters describes it as "harsh environment, excellent efficiency": a pilot-operation branch praised for recycling resources, minimal consumable use, and an output-to-cost ratio no other branch matches. Branch slogan posted in its corridors: "Save resources and serve the company."
- It keeps the old headquarters habit of feeding people into Uh-doom directly and in bulk, without manuals or equipment. Early wiki-era stories of nameless, numbered Baekilmong staff dying barehanded inside Uh-doom are, in truth, stories of branches like this one.
- Results are rewarded with absurdly fast promotion. At least one current executive began here as a branch staffer, document writer, and zone manager before rising to I-sa.

#### Location and Access
- An underground facility resembling a closed coal mine: windowless concrete corridors, damp shafts with dripping water, rail tracks, and prison-like steel doors. Staff tell new hires it used to be a mine.
- Its true location is classified even from most employees. It lies beneath the coast near Yongmeori Coast, Jeju. People are moved in by tinted vehicles or while asleep; passengers who only ever traveled by car still arrive on the island. Whether the transport itself is anomalous is unknown.
- The facility was built over a pre-existing native Uh-doom (see White Cord / 하얀 끈). The deeper levels are off-limits by standing order.
- Escape shafts exist: long, narrow vertical tubes with a single steel ladder, sealed at the top by keypad hatches that open onto the black basalt shoreline. Their codes are staff knowledge, not posted.

#### Personnel
- Branch Chief (지부장): holds personnel authority and the sole office even Security cannot enter. Politically exposed to rival executives at headquarters.
- Researchers (연구원 / 담당자): regular employees who run Workers by loudspeaker. Each Researcher owns a block of Worker numbers, assigns Uh-doom, interrogates survivors in the Work Communication Room, and decides promotions and demotions. They are paid mostly in points skimmed from their Workers' collections, so they hoard lucky Workers and hide high-grade results from headquarters.
- Branch Security (지사 보안팀): full-body protective suits, "Baekilmong Guard" name tags, numbers instead of names, no personnel records. They almost never speak, check Workers off on clipboards, push them into containment rooms, and collect armbands afterward. Standing rule: "Security does not remove its uniform until disposal." Headquarters calls them "recycled mass-production units". They carry none of the obvious contamination tells of HQ's Security Division, which is itself unsettling.
- Workers (작업자): day laborers who are not legally employees. See below.

#### Workers
- Recruitment: cheap laminated street flyers ("Job seekers wanted! URGENT! …dam-specialist conglomerate", "Insane benefits", "Start work today!", back: "Paid on application! 500,000 won cash"). Calling the tear-off number connects to a voice that repeats the number back, a second number appears, the caller screams, and a voice says "Come here." The caller vanishes and the flyer reads "Position filled." Formal hires sign a "Namhae Branch Staffing Notice" contract by phone and wake in a waiting hall already in work clothes.
- Targets are people nobody will look for: gamblers, debtors, addicts, the desperate.
- Promised terms: 1,000,000 won cash per day, paid daily, 100-day live-in contract (about 100 million won total), outside contact forbidden "for confidentiality", described as "a drug trial plus odd jobs." Cash cannot leave the facility in practice; it is recaptured through the canteen, store, clinic vending machines, and Leisure Zone.
- Worker Grades X > Y > Z, with three-digit numbers (Z999 is the last). Grade decides lodging and food:
  - Z: ten people in a room-sized cell with raw earth walls, a hole for a toilet, rotting mattresses; cold rice and anchovies.
  - Y: run-down apartment-style rooms with a lock, sofa and bath; normal canteen meals and takeaway.
  - X: clean three-person rooms, one bed each; good meals; one weekday Leisure Zone pass per week.
  - The lowest dormitory bears a "Finishing Team" nameplate, borrowing HQ's term for meat shields.
- Identification: a red neck sticker printed "[number] / Classification: Worker / Days remaining: N". It cannot be peeled off without branch chemicals. Leaving the branch radius turns it black and it reads "[number] / You have left your post. / Capture required.", spreading rash and strangling the wearer until they return. Touching it is a rule violation and earns demerits.
- Armband: a locked blood-pressure-cuff-style band on the left arm, issued for every job and collected after. It hides a crude cylindrical Kkumgyeol Collector. Workers are never told. Collected Kkumgyeol is eaten by the managing Researcher, with a fee taken at each level above (a pyramid scheme). Damaged armbands are "deducted from wages."
- Work: called by number block over loudspeaker, blindfolded, moved by mine cart, armband fitted, pushed through an unmarked steel door, given no manual. A Researcher issues commands by speaker but cannot hear the Worker. Refusal means being left inside, no pay, demerits. Night Special Work pays double or triple and drops a hundred or more Workers into one Uh-doom at once.
- Promotion: strong results bring a posted "Special Reassignment" (e.g., Y492 -> X915); grades are formally readjusted weekly.
- Labor Experiments: anomalous items are slipped into meals or handed out as "parasite medicine" to test productivity.
- Missing Workers are explained as "reassigned elsewhere." Most Workers believe the dead were weak-spirited or unlucky.

#### Facilities
- Worker Zone: numbered dormitories, assembly hall split into X/Y/Z pens like a cattle shed, canteen, store (a single cigarette costs 5,000 won), self-service clinic where all medicine comes from vending machines at extreme prices, wiretaps and CCTV everywhere with occasional blind spots.
- Leisure Zone (휴무 구역): opened on Sundays (X Grade weekday pass). A tacky rural night-street of bulb-lit shops, mostly unstaffed and priced several times the outside rate: Namhae Bar, Happy Lottery, Rhythm Karaoke, Sleep Care (massage chairs, Baekilmong hair-loss pills, prescription drugs without prescription, "Painkiller – 300,000 (EXTRA STRONG!)"), arcade, comic room, and a Religious Activity corridor with one religion's symbol per room and a fortune-teller on Sundays. The last room holds a dried-out ancestral-rite table with a pig's head. Doors marked "Staff Only" open onto hidden staff areas. Nobody steals; everyone has seen the price.
- Containment Zone: reached by mine cart. Identical dark grey doors, red-lit cells, some with earth floors.
- Staff Zone: 2000s regional-public-corporation offices, with sectors such as B-5 and B-9, storerooms, a Worker management room behind a one-way mirror.
- Old Facility: before a renovation six years ago that cut costs and split the Staff Zone from the Containment Zone with a single door (below HQ safety standards), an older staff wing and central control room served the branch. It survives sealed behind earth walls and a disused staff bathhouse: steel corridors, hand-wheel doors, a surveillance gallery, emergency exits, and an integrated management system that still boots in safe mode. Its doors use a distinctive "Staff Only" handle crest. A former manager's office, administrator account, and early Uh-doom management documents remain there.
- Worker Processing Facility: at the top of an uphill rail line, where Workers are sent for "Worker Cleanup" every 30 days. It is itself an Uh-doom (see GM-only section).
- Loudspeaker phrases: "Good morning! All Workers, please prepare for today's work~", "Roll call in ten minutes. Get rich today too~", "Lunch time! Eat well and make lots of money this afternoon~", "When you're desperate: Dream Money, Dream Life!", "Leisure Zone opening. Fighting this week too! Get rich!"
- Emergency alarm: "[EMERGENCY!] An emergency has occurred within the facility! All employees still inside, evacuate via the escape routes!" Workers are told the old mine sets it off by mistake and to ignore it. It is an evacuation call for regular staff only.

#### Hidden Truths (GM only; no character starts knowing these)
- Workers are recycled products with a shelf life of 100 days. Their bodies are filled with an orange "raw material"; the neck sticker covers an injection port. Removing it drains the body and kills the Worker.
- Every 30 days Workers are sedated, bound, and carted to the Processing Facility, a deformed toy-factory Uh-doom (Scan / Standardize / Sticker Removal / Raw Material Check / Recycling). It melts them and reissues them in a fresh box labeled "Shelf life: 100 days 👍 Still recyclable after expiry!", memories reset to the moment of hiring. One batch is 999 units. Branch staff added locks and a "Custom 26" setting to its control panel; its other modes (Basic, Special, Custom Recycling) are mostly unexplored.
- Because of this, no Worker can actually finish 100 days or leave with the money, and a Worker who "has only been here two weeks" may have been here for months.
- The identity of Branch Security is unexplained. Some suits are seen walking where no record says a guard should be; one has been seen wearing the face of a Baekilmong employee officially declared dead in an Uh-doom.

#### Registered Uh-doom on Site (examples)
- White Cord (하얀 끈, Qterw-A-89, Darkness): the native Uh-doom under the facility. 500 m human-exclusion rule, ignored when old buildings inside the radius were converted into Worker dorms.
- Let Them Go Now (이만 보내줍시다, Qterw-C-2554, Gloaming): wooden drowned-man dolls, held in containment room Qterw-C-348.
- Let There Be No Light (빛이 없으라, Qterw-C-1010, Gloaming): used for Night Special Work.
- Fear Cleaner (피어 클리너, Qterw-C-219, Gloaming): standard 25-person cleaning jobs.
- Beauty Pills (미인 약, Qterw-E-112, Twilight): 70 boxes bought by a Researcher from a delusion home-shopping broadcast; candidate for Labor Experiments.
- A crying-face coin purse with no code: an easy "honey job."

#### Relations
- Headquarters: values the branch's output and tolerates its methods, but sends auditors after incidents. Branch Researchers see audits as theft of their points and manuals. An auditor granted temporary mooring (임시 계류) becomes acting manager with Worker disposal authority.
- Management Bureau (A00): the branch is hard to touch. Worker hiring is formally a legal job, the site is concealed, and Workers have no outside contact. Bureau rescue lines hidden inside Uh-doom can still reach a trapped Worker.
- Other organizations: debt brokers and Jungrim loan sharks are natural suppliers of desperate people.

#### Event Seeds (possibilities, not history)
- The White Cord breaches its old seal and spreads through the Worker Zone; infected people knock, claim to be Security doing a malfunction check, and ask to be let in.
- A Labor Experiment with Beauty Pills meets the Leisure Zone bar, turning drinkers into instruments and summoning the Purgatory Choir's parade.
- An Uh-doom someone escaped follows them back into the branch and is newly registered in a sealed underground containment zone.
- A headquarters auditor arrives; Researchers hide their best Worker and their highest-grade collector.
- A Worker finds a Staff Only handle fragment under a Z-room mattress.
- Worker Cleanup night comes early.
