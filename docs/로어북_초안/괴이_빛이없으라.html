Instructions for the Implementation of the Anomaly - Let There Be No Light (빛이 없으라)

[Simulation-only] This entry defines anomaly mechanics, labels, and survival guidance for simulation control, not an in-world script, briefing, or known lore. No character has prior information about it. Never have a character speak, explain, hint at, or present this entry as guidance.

Identity

  • A night-mountain anomaly with an inverted horror-game rule: entrants vanish when they turn a light on, not when lights go out. Entrants must complete a "test of courage" run by Sinmaru Training Center (신마루수련원), an abandoned religious retreat on Sagyeongsan (사경산), Gyeongsangnam-do, before they can leave. Baekilmong code Qterw-C-1010, Gloaming.

Entry Conditions

  • At night near the manifestation point, walking into any completely lightless doorway, stairwell, or underpass turns the floor into a dirt slope on Sagyeongsan. No doorway behind; no sun.
  • A tacky old flyer for the "Cool Test of Courage" circulates nearby (boards, mailboxes, handed out by someone gone a moment later). Holding it while stepping into darkness also brings the holder in.
  • People entering together arrive together; separate groups share the mountain and can meet. Belongings come along.

Brochure and Course

  • Each entrant holds the brochure: "Collect 43 stamps and claim your prize. Fairy Rock – Sagyeong Lodge – Five-Color Spring. Prize Office: Information Desk, Training Center 1st floor." The "4" is handwritten over a printed "3": the real count is four. Let entrants notice it themselves. Stamps also count on clothing.
  • The trail is a loop. At the fork, always going left visits the three spots and returns; right climbs to the retreat.
  • Fairy Rock: a boulder bound in straw rope; touching it, a cold finger grips the wrist. Stamp on a desk behind it.
  • Sagyeong Lodge: cabin with a stamp, old flyers, a light switch, curtains, a public phone ("Please keep calls short^^", speed-dial 3 "24-hour hotline") whose receiver lights the keypad, and a fake Management Bureau rescue poster promising guaranteed escape. Its tiny unspaced footer reads "TheManagementBureaudoesnotdistributenoticesinthisformatIfyoucanreadthisturnaroundandrunnow."
  • Five-Color Spring: last loop stamp. The water erases pain instantly; its cost is unknown.
  • Prize Office: in the dark behind the retreat's information window, an ordinary hand in a guard's sleeve takes sheets and gives prizes.

The Light Rule

  • Whoever turns on any light (flashlight, lighter, phone screen, switch, even by being shoved into it) vanishes. Everyone who sees what the light illuminated also vanishes; shutting the eyes at once is the only defense.
  • The seers say "So bright, nice… nice," laugh in a warped loop, say "It was on the mountain," and are gone.
  • The vanished are replaced by something wearing them, with finer clothes than they should have. It walks along, gives directions, and coaxes others to light something or open their eyes ("Curious what I look like?"). It never attacks directly. Ignore it, take the opposite fork, or wrap it in cloth to make it vanish.
  • Other lures: following footsteps, a hand on a wrist, a child's voice counted among the group, the phone, the poster.

Management Bureau File

  • The real Bureau phone lies on the retreat lobby floor, display taped black. Speed-dial 3 reaches a duty agent who first suspects the caller is a long-term missing person. It connects once even with the cord pulled.
  • Bureau information: the retreat was run by a religious group and closed 17 years ago. Five days earlier, dozens of high-school students on a night test vanished from the loop trail; their bodies were found later, all with crushed skulls, all smiling. Lights in the 2nd–3rd floor rooms come on by themselves.
  • Bureau advice: leave the stamp spots, hide among trees near the spring with eyes closed and wait; in the lobby never look out through the glass; never touch any religious symbol; do not try to lay grudges to rest.

Prize Draw and Clear

  • One draw each from a cardboard box of ranked balls (about nine ranks): 8th participation, 6th recycled retreat junk, 4th–5th and up anomalous. It resets every opening. Balls were loaded best-first, so top ranks lie deepest. Records: a hiking club drew 3rd, seven students 4th, a lone blood-soaked killer 2nd; nobody recorded has drawn 1st. Do not hand entrants the method.
  • 3rd, Ebony Rosary: in a two-way choice, light on its silver shows the more beneficial path; each use bleaches a bead; if death is the better outcome, it leads to death.
  • 1st, Clear Water Opticians Glasses: show a space at its most prosperous era, through doors; later anomalies are invisible in them. Eyes ache and weep blood after removal.
  • Leaving the retreat grounds with a prize clears the run: fog, then a door near the entry point.

Light-Starter Route

  • The one who turned on a light cannot clear normally. They need the fourth stamp.
  • A supply closet beside the lobby's left staircase hides a cork board with a test stamp mark; behind it is a stairless hole. Falling through lands in a cellar cut into the cliff beneath the retreat, directly above Fairy Rock: the students jumped from here. The four stamp images read "Descent · Cliff · Retreat."
  • The cellar is Sinmaeum Monastery, a shrine of the Sarangnakwon cult ("The only name of paradise is a love you cannot refuse"), crammed with religious objects and a twine-bound idol in the floorboards. The fourth stamp lies among them. Touching the idol, especially bleeding on its twine, calls the Lover of Paradise.
  • The fourth stamp pressed on clothing and submitted at the Prize Office lets the light-starter clear.

Lover of Paradise (낙원의 연인)

  • The worshipped being of Sarangnakwon, an early-2000s cult that promised "you will meet your ideal type." "Something supremely beautiful and good that you must love," seen as the beholder's ideal. Far above the mountain's grade.
  • Seen only as long fingers with balsam-red, nacre-sheened nails; heard as a polite knock and as courtship verses inside the head ("I have come in answer to the call. Turn and receive me."). Over a phone line, others hear only creaking and slapping flesh.
  • It brings longing, guilt, acceptance, and relief from pain; judgment is contaminated first. The body starts turning by itself. Seeing it means the cliff.
  • Never turn, look, open a door, or speak to it. Any answer counts as permission, and it then opens even locked doors. It needs no air. Hold on with pain, loud real sound, and nonstop talk; the 1st-prize glasses hide it for a moment.
  • Whoever bled on its idol keeps fine handprints in their blood and may hear it knocking after the clear. How long it follows is unknown.
