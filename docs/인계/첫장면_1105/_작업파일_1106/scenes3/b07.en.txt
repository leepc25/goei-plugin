=== P043
9:10 in the morning. Yeongdeungpo. Special Disaster Response Association, Mission Coordination Office.

The copier was running. Five in the waiting seats. Unassigned postings scrolled up and down on the wall monitor.

ㅡScrape.

A man sat down in the chair at the intake desk. Twenty-something. Cable ties instead of shoelaces. The face that came in for the first time last week. The name was memorized on the second visit.

"Oh, you're back. The one who talked about ramyeon last week."

<img=Cha Yeo-jin_smile>

"…You remember?"

"Of course. Sit, sit. Want coffee? Not the office stuff, mine."

A page turned at the next desk.

<img=Seo Yun-ji_bored>

"Registration number. Then we will proceed in document order."

"Yun-ji, he hasn't even sat down yet."

"He has."

The man gave his registration number. While Seo Yun-ji looked it up, a drawer opened. One file. 'Sora Land' on the cover.

"This one. I don't show this to just anyone."

The voice dropped a notch.

<img=Cha Yeo-jin_smirk>

"Theme park night inspection. Go in after closing, recover one mascot head. The client's in a hurry, so the fee went up. Done before lunch… no, before dawn."

"The risk grade is high."

"Paperwork inflation. The place is genuinely fine in the daytime. They sell churros."

"This posting is night entry."

Seo Yun-ji. Eyes still on her screen.

<img=Seo Yun-ji_serious>

"The reason for the grade increase is 'no repeat-visit reviews after closing'. Payment is upon confirmed receipt of the recovered item. No payment in case of disappearance. Clause 3."

"…I was going to explain."

"I have read you Clause 3."

The man looked from the file to the two of them. His hand fidgeting with the cable tie was visible.

The phone lying face down on the desk buzzed. Not the registered one. 'Jackpot' flashed on the screen and went dark.

<img=Cha Yeo-jin_annoyed>

"…I'll take it later."

"You may take it now. I will continue the intake."

"Yun-ji."

"From Clause 4."

<img=Seo Yun-ji_expressionless>

Seo Yun-ji turned the fee table toward the man. Smoothed a folded corner with her finger.

"The commission is 15 percent by grade. The handwritten note on the file says 40. Coordinator Cha, which number do I read to him."

The man looked up.

<img=Cha Yeo-jin_forced smiling>

"…Coordinator. If I do this, how much do I actually keep?"

=== P044
9:40 in the morning. Special Disaster Response Association, Mission Coordination Office. The right-hand intake desk.

The wristwatch matched the building clock. Set on Monday.

A woman sat in the chair across the desk. It took her 11 seconds to say her registration number. Because she was crying.

"Registration number. Then we will proceed in document order."

"…Yesterday. I signed here yesterday. Two of us. Lookymart."

<img=Seo Yun-ji_expressionless>

"Yesterday's intake was the left desk."

"That person isn't here, right now."

The left desk was empty. Cha Yeo-jin was in the smoking room. Twenty minutes now.

"My boyfriend didn't come out. The closing announcement played and… only I came out. From the 4th floor. They say that mart has no 4th floor. The documents said no…"

"The documents state 'three floors above ground'."

"Then where was I."

Someone in the waiting seats cleared their throat. The copier stopped and started again.

The woman's registration number was looked up. Intake yesterday afternoon. Processing code: Cha Yeo-jin. A correction mark in the risk-grade field. Recorded.

<img=Seo Yun-ji_serious>

"Posting fees are paid upon confirmed receipt of the recovered item. In a two-person contract, if one does not return, the fee is not paid in full to the returnee. Clause 7. The non-returnee is processed as client error, and the commission is deducted as is."

"…You're reading me that right now?"

"Yes."

ㅡClick-clack.

Shoes came from the smoking room side. Cha Yeo-jin sat down, flipping her hair over her shoulder.

<img=Cha Yeo-jin_surprised>

"Oh? Yesterday's… you're back. Alone?"

The woman looked at Cha Yeo-jin. The crying stopped.

"Ah, such a shame. He had real promise."

<img=Cha Yeo-jin_smile>

"Anyway, are you okay? Yun-ji, this is my intake. Hand me the file, would you? I'll finish it."

"The processing code is yours, Coordinator Cha."

"Right. So."

"There is a correction mark in the risk-grade field. The correction time and reason are blank. They must be filled in before transfer."

<img=Cha Yeo-jin_annoyed>

"…Yun-ji."

The woman looked between the two desks. The copy of yesterday's contract was crumpled in her hand.

<img=Seo Yun-ji_bored>

"Shall I read the next clause, or do you require a moment. The session resumes either way."

"…Just one thing."

The woman looked straight at Seo Yun-ji.

"That correction mark. Did you see it yesterday too?"

=== P045
10 in the morning. Jungnim Underground. Son-gil Ui-won, exam room 2.

Antiseptic smell. Fluorescent light.

ㅡKnock knock.

"Doctor, the patient's coming in. Slowly, slowly."

Yun Ji-a held the door. A man came in. Left side pressed with his hand. Something black showed between his fingers. Not blood.

<img=Yun Ji-a_default>

"Have you eaten anything today? Want a candy at least?"

"…No thanks."

"Then some water. Here."

The man sat in the chair. Each breath, his left chest rose less than the right.

"Two ribs. A collapsed lung. And whatever that black tissue is."

"…Gangseo."

"Gangseo University Hospital?"

"Building B. The practice building. I got out of there. It took three days."

<img=Yun Ji-a_worried>

Yun Ji-a stopped setting down the water cup and looked at the man.

"I can fix all three. Payment first."

"…I don't have money."

"Then I can't fix them."

"I can't breathe right now."

"I know. That's why I told you the amount."

The man looked at Yun Ji-a. Yun Ji-a looked at the doctor.

<img=Yun Ji-a_pleading>

"Doctor… um, at least some oxygen…"

"Oxygen is extra too, Ji-a."

ㅡThud. Thud. Thud.

Steel-toed boots in the corridor. The door opened. Leather jacket. Long black hair slicked back.

<img=Shin Yuri_default>

"Doctor. This one's my client. He came by basement two this morning."

"Ms. Shin. Knock."

"I did. If you didn't hear it, that's your problem."

Shin Yuri stood beside the man. Didn't sit. The man couldn't lift his head.

"You. You have no money. You have one usable talent and two kidneys."

<img=Shin Yuri_contemptuous>

"…The talent… they did something at Gangseo… it doesn't work right now."

"Not working and not having are different."

Shin Yuri turned toward the doctor.

"Doctor. One question. If you take that black stuff out, does his talent come back? If it does, I take it as collateral and pay the surgery. Ten percent, weekly. If it doesn't, the kidney."

<img=Yun Ji-a_scared>

"…Ms. Shin, in front of the patient…"

"The patient needs to hear it. So he can't say he was surprised later."

Shin Yuri put a hand on the desk. The sheath at the small of her back showed under the jacket for a moment.

<img=Shin Yuri_serious>

"So. Does it come back, or not?"

=== P046
11 in the morning. Jungnim Underground. Son-gil Ui-won reception window.

Two in the waiting room. Plastic chairs. Old magazines.

One man sat with a bandage on his left arm. Sutured yesterday. His right hand was inside his jacket. What he was holding was clear from the shape.

"Excuse me. Have you eaten anything today?"

"…No."

"Want a candy? There's grape and lemon."

<img=Yun Ji-a_smile>

The man looked at the window for a moment. His right hand came out of the jacket. Empty. He took the lemon.

"Stitches come out tomorrow. Today the doctor just takes one look and sends you home."

ㅡClick-clack.

The other one stood up. Burgundy suit. Black bob. Not an unfamiliar face. The owner of Heol-gap Mae-ip around the corner of the passage.

<img=Ma Deok-hee_smile>

"Ji-a, right? The kid assistant."

"…Yes. Are you here for a consult?"

"No. I'm here to see you."

Ma Deok-hee rested her arm on the window. The glasses stayed hooked at her chest pocket.

"The clients here mostly come when their money's about to run out. Some leave without paying for surgery. And later that kind of person comes to my shop. With a watch or a ring."

"…Yes."

"I'd like to know a day earlier, that's all. Who left without paying. Skip the name, just the time and what they were carrying."

<img=Yun Ji-a_confused>

"That's… patient information."

"It's information, which is why I'd pay. This much per item."

Three fingers. A number that knew Yun Ji-a's salary.

"I don't do that."

"'I don't do that.' I don't do that. Mm."

<img=Ma Deok-hee_smirk>

"You dropped out of nursing school because you couldn't pay tuition. You say you'll apply again next term. Every month."

Yun Ji-a's hand stopped on the pouch zipper.

<img=Yun Ji-a_sad>

"…Who says that."

"The clients. Lots of people here are grateful. The kid assistant who gives water and talks to them. They don't talk much about the doctor."

ㅡClack.

The exam room door opened. White coat. The antiseptic smell came out first.

<img=Go Yeon-ji_default>

"Ji-a. Prep room 3. Yesterday's suture patient first."

"…Yes, Doctor."

"Ms. Ma. If it's not a consult, please clear the waiting seat."

"Going, going."

Ma Deok-hee took her arm off the window. The dimple showed on her left cheek.

<img=Ma Deok-hee_contemptuous>

"I'll come again next week, Ji-a. You won't tell the doctor, will you?"
