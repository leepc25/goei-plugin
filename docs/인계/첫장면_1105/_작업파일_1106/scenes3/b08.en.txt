=== P050
10 in the morning. Seodaemun. The Christian goods shop.

Crosses on the shelves. Rosaries. Bibles. Under the counter, other things.

ㅡJingle.

A man came in. A scarf around his neck. A scarf in October means one of two things. Cold, or something to hide.

"Welcome! Nice to meet you. Blaret, special-class nun of the Papal Court. Dispatched to Korea on vampire extermination business."

<img=Blaret_happy>

"…Sorry?"

"Joking. What are you looking for?"

"A cross. The… strongest one."

"The strongest. A fun customer."

The man adjusted his scarf in front of the shelf. In that moment, the scent came. Not from the customer. From the customer's neck. A known scent. Very old.

"Been on the subway? Lately."

"…How did you know."

"The last train?"

"…Yes. Some woman, in a red dress, said she was locked in, so I opened the door for her, and…"

<img=Blaret_serious>

"And you don't remember after that."

"…No."

"Could you show me your neck?"

The man lowered the scarf. Two bite marks. The wounds were clean.

"Take this one. It's silver."

<img=Blaret_smile>

"How much…"

"On the house today. In exchange, one thing. You don't believe in that Islam business, do you? How about coming over to our side?"

"I don't believe in anything."

"Then it's even easier."

ㅡBang.

The door flew open. No time for the bell. White turban. Black suit. Rifle on the back. Scimitar at the waist.

<img=Saladin_happy>

"Brother!"

"…Sorry?"

Saladin took both the man's hands. The man nearly fell over backward.

"Nun. I saw a brother enter the heathen's shop. I have come to save him."

"Save him? He's my customer right now. And I'm open for business."

"Brother! Do not lend your ear to the heathen's words!"

<img=Blaret_annoyed>

The man pulled his scarf back up between the two of them. Saladin's hands stopped.

"…Brother. That neck."

"You saw? Yes. The last train."

Saladin let go of the man's hands. His hand went to the scimitar's hilt. Under the counter, this hand moved too. Both stopped there.

<img=Saladin_serious>

"Nun. You have seen what is on this brother's neck. It means that woman is in this city."

"I know. It's my job."

"If it is your job, why is she still alive."

"…Because you keep getting in the way?"

<img=Saladin_angry>

"Brother! Ride the last train with me tonight! I will stand at your side!"

<img=Blaret_smirk>

"Sir. He's got a rifle and I've got silver bullets. Which do you think is quieter on a subway?"

The man clutched the cross. His hand shook.

"…I, I just came to buy a cross. Do I really have to ride the last train tonight?"

=== P051
9 in the morning. Itaewon. The back courtyard of the Seoul Central Mosque.

Morning prayer was done. The scimitar had been polished. The rifle lay disassembled in a row.

ㅡPatter patter.

A young man came running in. The brother adopted last month. Too out of breath to speak.

"Brother! Catch your breath first."

<img=Saladin_happy>

"Hyung-nim… the cathedral. The cathedral in our neighborhood…"

"A cathedral? That is a house of the heathen."

"It… turned into flies."

"Flies?"

"The building. The doors became like fly wings, and the floor is… the floor is a belly. An insect belly. The sky is red. Crows are circling. People are inside and they're not coming out."

The rifle assembly stopped. He looked at the brother's face. Not the eyes of someone lying. The eyes of someone saying what he saw.

<img=Saladin_serious>

"The Lord of Flies."

"…You know it?"

"Beelzebub. A wicked thing called the king of flies. I have heard it takes a heathen cathedral as its body. The people inside…"

"…"

"Brother. Is there someone you know inside the cathedral?"

"My mother."

The assembly resumed. The hands moved faster. The explosive pouches went across the chest.

"The evil god will burn there."

<img=Saladin_determined>

ㅡClick-clack.

Shoes at the courtyard gate. Not high heels. The nun wore combat boots.

"Good morning. I heard the rumor."

<img=Blaret_smile>

Blonde. Veil. The habit slit at the side. Beneath it, a holster.

"Nun. This is a mosque."

"I know. That's why I didn't come inside. This is the courtyard."

"What brings you."

"That cathedral. It was ours originally. A Catholic cathedral, before it became flies. So I'm going."

"My brother's mother is inside."

<img=Blaret_surprised>

"Oh my. Then that's one more reason to go together."

"There will be no going together with a heathen."

"You said that last time too, and we went together."

A hand went to the scimitar. The nun's hand went down toward her thigh. Both stopped there.

<img=Saladin_annoyed>

"…A temporary alliance. Temporary only."

"Yes, yes. You say that every time."

The brother stood between them, not knowing what to do.

<img=Blaret_curious>

"So. I'm ready now."

"Hyung-nim… my mother…"

<img=Saladin_worried>

"Brother, a little longer. Nun. I will stand before the flies first."

One painting on the wall. A painting that hurts whoever looks at it.

"I want that glass."
