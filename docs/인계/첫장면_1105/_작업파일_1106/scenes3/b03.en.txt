=== P013
One in the afternoon. Bureau headquarters, Inspection Unit office.

The door opened and thirty people stood up at once.

"Department Head is here!"

One jerk of the chin and they all sat.

In the cage on the desk, the hamster was running its wheel. Song Ju-ik had changed the water this morning, it seemed. Fresh sawdust.

"Department Head."

<img=Go Young-hoon_default>

Go Young-hoon came and stood beside him. Sunglasses. Black suit. No expression.

"Report."

"Subway. Monkey Dream. Fourth missing person yesterday. All last trains."

"And."

"The operator has changed. Presumed vampire group. The monkey conductor is the same, but instead of dying at the next station, they're dragged out. Blood is being collected."

Go Young-hoon held out his terminal.

"Check the database. Code #440217, case 3."

A photo of a subway car on the screen. Passengers with blank faces. Among them, a monkey in uniform. And beyond the door at the end of the car, the hem of a red dress, half in frame.

<img=Go Young-hoon_serious>

"There is a sighting report of a True Ancestor bloodline individual. Unconfirmed. Suppression Unit handles the ghoul swarm. We take the individual."

"Hm."

The hamster in the cage came off the wheel. It looked toward Lee Do-hyeong and twitched its nose.

Then the office door burst open.

"Department Head! I brought the rookie!"

<img=No Gi-gyun_excited>

No Gi-gyun. Parade-ground voice, even indoors. A young man followed him in. The face that came up from the interrogation room yesterday. The suit didn't fit him yet.

"Let us depart for the world of the sword! Go on, greet him!"

"H-hello! Rookie…"

"Skip the name."

At Lee Do-hyeong's one line, the rookie shut his mouth. No Gi-gyun scratched his head.

<img=No Gi-gyun_embarrassed>

"Ah, Department Head. Still, a name at least…"

"I'll memorize it when his sense awakens. Until then he's an extra."

Go Young-hoon took back the terminal.

<img=Go Young-hoon_thinking>

"Whether to include the rookie is your decision. We don't usually put rookies on vampires for a first operation. But Deputy No says he did well in yesterday's verification sparring."

"It wasn't just well! He took my blade twice! Twice!"

<img=No Gi-gyun_proud>

No Gi-gyun shouted. Behind him, the rookie went red.

Go Young-hoon looked at Lee Do-hyeong.

The hamster was standing with its front paws against the cage wall. Facing Lee Do-hyeong.

<img=Go Young-hoon_expressionless>

"What about the rookie."

=== P015
11 in the morning. Bureau headquarters, underground training hall.

"Let us depart for the world of the sword!"

No Gi-gyun's voice hit the ceiling and came back. The dozen or so swordsmen leaning on the training hall wall covered their ears, used to it.

<img=No Gi-gyun_excited>

Two rookies. Came up from the interrogation room yesterday. One was tall, one had small eyes. Both had hands that had never held a wooden sword.

"Ju-ik! Pick out a sword for each and tell them how training works!"

"Yes! Ah, no, that's supposed to be the youngest's…"

<img=Song Ju-ik_annoyed>

Song Ju-ik grumbled, but was already carrying two wooden swords over. Chin first. Song Ju-ik was a man whose chin arrived first.

"Here, take them. This one's the right length, this one's a bit long for you. Turn your wrist. Yeah, that one."

The two rookies took the swords. The tall one asked.

"Deputy, when do we get real swords?"

"When your sense awakens. When the Department Head says 'not bad'."

"When is that?"

"I haven't heard it yet either."

<img=Song Ju-ik_sad>

Song Ju-ik's chin dropped a little. The swordsmen on the wall snickered.

"Quiet! Sparring begins!"

No Gi-gyun raised his sword. The tall rookie stepped forward.

ㅡCrack. Crack. Crack.

Three exchanges. The rookie's sword went flying.

"Anyone can see you never learned properly! But those feet! Your feet are good! Fix just that and you'll make deputy in no time!"

<img=No Gi-gyun_laughing>

"Next!"

The small-eyed rookie stepped up. The hand holding the sword was shaking.

ㅡCrack.

One exchange. No Gi-gyun's sword stopped. The tip of the rookie's sword was touching No Gi-gyun's flank.

The training hall went quiet.

<img=No Gi-gyun_dumbfounded>

"…Huh?"

The rookie himself looked startled.

"S-sorry! I didn't mean to…"

"No. No. Again."

ㅡCrack. Crack.

Two exchanges. No Gi-gyun won this time. But No Gi-gyun's face was not smiling.

<img=No Gi-gyun_thinking>

"…Where did you learn."

"I didn't. Never did kendo either."

"You were aiming for my flank just now, right? A cut there kills."

"…Just. I could see it there."

The swordsmen on the wall looked at each other. Song Ju-ik's chin came up.

<img=Song Ju-ik_surprised>

"Deputy. Is this maybe…"

"Be quiet."

No Gi-gyun lowered his sword. And looked at the rookie for a long while.

"Hey. What's your name."

"Ah, um, yesterday the Department Head said skip the name…"

"Not to the Department Head. To me."

The rookie said his name. No Gi-gyun rolled the name once in his mouth.

<img=No Gi-gyun_serious>

"Ju-ik."

"Yes."

"Where's the Department Head right now."

"Out hunting. Back this afternoon."

"The Deputy Director."

"Meeting."

Song Ju-ik looked from No Gi-gyun to the rookie and back. Then asked carefully.

"Deputy. Are you going to report this? You know how upstairs reacts when sense comes up on day one. Last time, that kid…"

"…What happened to that kid last time?"

The small-eyed rookie asked. Nobody answered.

Song Ju-ik looked at No Gi-gyun.

"Deputy. That kid from last time."

=== P016

"I'm right here, you bastard!"

"Cho Seong-choon! What are you doing there alone! Fall back!"

"If I fall back, who holds it!"

ㅡScreech.

A black van stopped on the road behind the line. No plates. The door opened and a woman in a nun's habit got out. Blonde. Two pistols on her thighs.

<img=Kwak Seong-ju_surprised>

"Hey, you! Don't shoot the van! The Vatican side isn't a target! Just watch!"

Kwak Seong-ju grabbed the megaphone and shouted. A few men lowered their muzzles.

"Excuse me! There's a True Ancestor bloodline down there! The ghouls are bait! The main body is on the platform! I'm going down, so you'll hold the top, right?"

<img=Kwak Seong-ju_annoyed>

"What's that woman saying!"

"She says hold the top! She's going down!"

"Alone?!"

"Alone!"

The nun drew a cross. The cross lengthened. It became a sword glowing white. The men's mouths fell open.

Cho Seong-choon ran to Kwak Seong-ju's side. Panting.

<img=Cho Seong-choon_nervous>

"Hey, Kwak Seong-ju. You going to follow that woman down? None of our guys who went underground came back alive. But if we send her alone, the Vatican takes all the credit for the True Ancestor."

<img=Cho Seong-choon_scared>

"That's not the bait, that's the main body coming up, isn't it."

"Excuse me! If you shoot now, you hit me too!"
