=== P001
Morning.

Through the Jongno rush-hour crowd and into the Bureau building.

Elevator to the third floor. Integrated Response Team.

Open the door, and the smell of coffee came first.

ㅡClick-clack.

"Oh, Team Leader. You're in early today?"

Jaemtri turned around, tray in hand. Beige long waves, smiling eyes. Four paper cups on the tray. She picked one up and held it out toward Jyaldi.

<img=Jaemtri_smile>

"Black."

Three stacks of overnight reports on the desk. And one sticky note on the monitor.

[Team Leader, how about pasta for tonight's team dinner? -Not Rojemon]

"Ah, that."

Jaemtri was already beside the desk.

<img=Jaemtri_happy>

"Only one person in this office writes 'not Rojemon'. Hehe. The dinner menu is your call, Team Leader. I'll make a note."

First report. A multi-family house in Seongbuk-gu. Last night. Two survivors, no deaths. The Suppression Team had sealed the building first, and Kyuras wrote the report. Kyuras's reports were, as always, needlessly long. The second was a revised draft of the field rulebook. The third was the rookie training rota.

In the box for next week, Spangdal's name was written in.

"Ah, you saw that?"

Jaemtri peered at the rota, and her eyes folded into half-moons.

"Spangdal said that week was her quietest. She wrote herself in. Hehe. If you're worried, I'll stand next to her and watch. I can cover her mouth, at least."

Then.

"Who's talking about me?"

<img=Spangdal_smirk>

Without a sound, Spangdal was standing in the doorway. Purple wavy bob. Eyes with no light in them.

"Rookie training. I'm taking it, right?"

Spangdal's mouth curled up, slowly.

<img=Spangdal_smug>

"Don't worry. I won't kill anyone. They all died on their own. I never killed a single one."

"Spangdal."

"Joking, joking. Hehehe."

Spangdal went to her seat and sat. Even as she sat, her eyes stayed on Jyaldi. The eyes of someone watching a fish in a tank.

Jaemtri sighed.

<img=Jaemtri_annoyed>

"That's why the Suppression Team won't take her either."

"I can hear you."

"I said it so you'd hear."

ㅡDing.

The terminal on the desk chimed once.

Jaemtri looked at the screen first. Her smile went out for just a moment, then came back on.

<img=Jaemtri_serious>

"Team Leader. Eunpyeong-gu."

She turned the pad around.

"Missing-person reports came in all at once. Seven people from the same apartment in one night. An old five-story corridor block. There's a post on the World Politics Forum too."

The post was on the screen.

[Our apartment elevator has one extra button now. The guy next door pressed it and didn't come back. I didn't press it but the doors opened and it was the 5th floor. The corridor wall is lukewarm. I'm hiding in the playground right now. Battery 12%. Not a joke.]

Over thirty comments. Most said it was fake. A few asked which apartment. The poster hadn't answered.

"It's Myeongil Apartment. One extra button, cross the threshold and it's the fifth floor, wall is lukewarm. All three match."

"Only the poster is still alive. The other six are out of contact. I sent a message. No reply yet."

"Sounds fun."

Spangdal said it from her seat. She had pulled a black thorn from her fingertip and was shaping it like a nail file.

<img=Spangdal_curious>

"That place hasn't gone out in fifteen years. I always wanted to go down there once."

"Spangdal, you need to prepare rookie training."

"That's next week."

Jaemtri ignored her and tapped the pad.

"I've already contacted Aileen. The trio is on standby too. Should I put the Suppression Team on alert? Oh, and."

Jaemtri lowered her voice a little.

<img=Jaemtri_smug>

"Konjyuce has been pacing the corridor for a while. She saw you come in and wants to tag along. What do we do with her this time?"

Then Jaemtri's pad chimed once more.

"One moment."

The smile left Jaemtri's face as she read.

Under the post, a comment, just now.

[OP) Someone is coming to the playground. Looks like the security guard but it's not the security guard. What do I do]

The battery indicator had dropped to 11 percent.

<img=Jaemtri_nervous>

"Team Leader. Twenty minutes by car. And the battery is at 11 percent."

=== P002
Two in the afternoon. Bureau headquarters, basement level one. The firing range.

ㅡBang. Bang. Bang.

Muffled gunfire kept coming through the ear protection. Three rookies this intake. All three were shooting at targets, but their eyes kept drifting backward. Spangdal was standing in the shadow behind the booths. Much closer than they thought.

"Shoot. Don't look at me."

The rookie in the leftmost booth was changing a magazine when his hands shook.

ㅡClatter.

The magazine hit the floor and rolled. Where it stopped, the toe of Spangdal's shoe was already waiting.

<img=Spangdal_smirk>

The rookie couldn't lift his head.

"S-sorry!"

"It's fine. It's fine. If you'd dropped it in the field, it wouldn't have been."

The rookie in the second booth was at least watching the target. Seven out of ten. The rookie in the third booth, four out of ten. All four dead center in the chest.

"Senior! I got them all in the chest this time!"

The third rookie turned around and grinned. A proud face.

<img=Spangdal_bored>

"Yeah. I saw. The chest."

The rookie's grin stiffened a little.

"Hey. While you were shooting the chest, where did the other six go? Ever thought about where six missed shots go in the field?"

<img=Spangdal_smug>

"Hehe. Joking. Shoot again."

Meanwhile, high heels came down the corridor beyond the glass.

ㅡClick-clack.

The range door opened and Jaemtri put her head in. No tray. A pad instead. The moment she stepped in, she smiled and put a finger to her lips.

<img=Jaemtri_smile>

"Spangdal. Mouth."

"I haven't said anything yet."

"You were about to."

Jaemtri looked over the three rookies, then back at Spangdal.

"Hehe. I came to see if you were bullying our cute rookies. And one more thing."

She held out the pad. The trio's field report was on the screen.

"The trio went into Saetbyeol Goshiwon. They're in front of room 203 now. Deadpole is asking for one more person. Says the corridor is longer than the rulebook."

<img=Jaemtri_thinking>

"I'd go, but I'm on desk duty. Aileen is out. The Team Leader is in a meeting."

Jaemtri looked straight at Spangdal. The smiling eyes didn't change.

"So. Spangdal, I'd like you to go. You know that place best, don't you?"

The third rookie carefully raised a hand.

"U-um. If you go, Senior, then we…"

"You keep shooting. Ten rounds, eyes closed."

Jaemtri answered for her, and looked at Spangdal again.

<img=Jaemtri_curious>

"Well? Will you go? Or take the rookies and let them watch from outside the goshiwon. You're the training lead next week anyway. Good to show them a site early."

"Or I tell Deadpole we have nobody. Then the three of them handle it."

ㅡDing.

Jaemtri's pad chimed. She looked at the screen and raised her eyebrows.

<img=Jaemtri_surprised>

"Oh my. It's from Deadpole."

[Jaemtri… there's knocking inside room 203… from inside… the rule sheet didn't say anything about knocking… Rojemon is holding my arm so I can't go in… and Geshugan is counting the end of the corridor…]

Jaemtri hugged the pad to her chest.

<img=Jaemtri_happy>

"Spangdal. Knocking, she says. From inside."

=== P003
7:50 in the morning. Integrated Response Team pantry.

The coffee machine was pouring the last cup. Five paper cups on the tray. Two black, three with condensed milk. The black ones were for the Team Leader and Spangdal.

ㅡHiss.

The machine stopped. When Jaemtri carried the tray out into the office, the people at their desks lifted their heads one by one.

"Coffee…? Thank you. I'm always in your debt."

<img=Deadpole_shy>

Deadpole took his cup and bowed three times. In the next seat, the one facing the door, Geshugan was hammering a report with two index fingers. When the cup was set down, he took it without looking up.

"…Mm."

<img=Geshugan_default>

Rojemon, leaning on the wall, grumbled the moment she got hers.

"Too much condensed milk."

"So you won't drink it?"

"…I'll drink it."

<img=Rojemon_pouting>

Rojemon took a sip and leaned on the wall. The Team Leader and Spangdal hadn't come in yet.

The desk seat. Opening the laptop brought up last night's screen, untouched. A secondhand trading app. An overseas shopping site. The World Politics Forum. New posts had piled up overnight.

Brain parasite posts. President mind-control posts. Reptilian sightings. Same as always.

"Shopping again?"

Deadpole was beside the desk with his cup. Round eyes.

"…N-no. It's patrol. Sorry. I did it again…"

"Um, that one."

Deadpole pointed at a corner of the screen.

<img=Deadpole_curious>

[Secondhand) Vintage postcard set for sale. Weird writing in the recipient box. Found it while moving so going cheap.]

In the photo, tiny letters repeated along the top of the postcard. Tuesday Quiz Show. Tuesday Quiz Show. Tuesday Quiz Show. The letters in the recipient box were garbled.

"Isn't that… a broadcast application postcard? Brownie's."

Seller profile. Three trade reviews. Last online four minutes ago. While the chat window was being opened, Deadpole added carefully,

"It goes without a stamp. You just have to write on it. It's in the rulebook…"

"Don't talk nonsense. A civilian writes their name and that's it. What rulebook?"

<img=Rojemon_annoyed>

Rojemon said it from the wall. Deadpole shrank.

"S-sorry."

"Not you. I meant that seller."

Then a reply appeared in the chat window. The seller.

[Yes, I still have it! But I wrote my name on one yesterday as a joke lol. Is that okay?]

The office went quiet. Geshugan's typing stopped too.

<img=Geshugan_serious>

"…Yesterday."

Geshugan said it short.

"Today is Tuesday."

Rojemon came off the wall. She set her snack bag on the desk and peered at the laptop screen.

"If the broadcast is tonight, doesn't that person get taken tonight?"

Deadpole set his cup down. His hand shook a little.

<img=Deadpole_determined>

"I can go. If we get the seller's address. I'll go and bring back every postcard…"

"You're not going alone."

Rojemon grabbed Deadpole's arm.

<img=Rojemon_worried>

"Stay next to me."

[Also since yesterday I keep getting texts from an unknown number. Saying to look forward to a fun Tuesday night. That's spam right?]

<img=Rojemon_serious>

"Jaemtri. The Team Leader isn't here yet. And it's Tuesday."

=== P005
5:10 in the afternoon. Bureau headquarters, underground parking.

Oiling the motorcycle chain. A frame close to two meters, crouched down, looked like one more pillar in the parking lot. Beside him, Kyuras had his helmet in hand and had not stopped talking.

<img=Kyuras_excited>

"So I went in with zerglings to harass the natural, right. And he goes and builds a bunker right there. At that timing."

"So you lost."

"I lost. But that rookie, he's wasted on the Integrated Response Team. If I win the next game, he's coming to Suppression."

"And if you lose?"

"I clean their pantry."

Kyuras said it, then paused.

<img=Kyuras_scared>

"…Wait. Their pantry is where Senior Spangdal is."

"Right."

"Ah, damn. Didn't think of that."

The color drained from Kyuras's face. Kyuras was not a person who hid that. He was not a person who could.

ㅡBeep-beep. Beep-beep.

Both terminals went off at once. The Suppression Team dispatch alert had its own sound. Short, low, twice.

[Suppression Team, immediate dispatch. Residential cathedral, Eunpyeong-gu. Sky red, crows circling. Trees dead outside the cathedral wall. Contamination spreading. Residents not evacuated.]

Kyuras put his helmet on.

<img=Kyuras_serious>

"A cathedral, so it's that one. Beelzebub. If it's started spreading past the wall, we're already late. Fifteen minutes to Eunpyeong. Let's go."

ㅡVroom!

The motorcycle hit the ramp and burst out into the empty Jongno inside the veil. The traffic lights changed by themselves.

Eunpyeong-gu. Visible from a distance. Only the sky above the cathedral spire was red, like a sunset. Crows circled above it. Closer, the sound came. Flies. Not one or two.

Residents had gathered in the alley in front of the cathedral wall. Every tree in the yard inside was dead, and the street trees beyond the wall were half dead. And the cathedral's front door was slightly open. The door swelled and sank, like breathing.

<img=Kyuras_nervous>

"Look at that. Even the street trees outside the wall are dead. They said those were fine yesterday. So it's spreading ten meters a day."

Kyuras said it from the back seat. Before the motorcycle even stopped, he had read the whole situation.

"Why are there so many onlookers. Hey, that kid with the phone went all the way to the front door. Is he insane."

Among the crowd that had come to watch, a young man had his phone raised and was filming through the gap in the open door.

ㅡBzzzz.

Something flew out through the gap. A fly the size of a pug. The young man screamed and fell backward.

Kyuras tore off his helmet and raised a finger.

<img=Kyuras_determined>

"Gorgodio, I can pop that fly right now. But if I pop it in front of the door, everything inside wakes up."

"You request containment. If I do it, HQ asks me three times over."

"And that kid with the phone has to come out too. And the wall has to be blocked. Can't do both."

The front door opened a little more. Red light leaked from inside.

Kyuras's finger hung in the air.

<img=Kyuras_angry>

"Gorgodio. Where do I pop it?"

=== P006

"…she smiled."

<img=Kyuras_scared>

"Kyuras, hello?"

<img=Spangdal_smirk>

<img=Spangdal_happy>

"Want me to come?"
