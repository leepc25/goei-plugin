Seven in the evening, at the foot of the back stairs down into the basement in Jungrim-dong. Beneath the faded sign of some old hospital, the door of Songil Clinic swung open.

Go Yeon-ji was in the exam room, pulling on her gloves. Her hair was swept back behind her ears and pinned in place, and the surname embroidered on the breast pocket of her coat was, as ever, without a speck on it. She had already wiped down the operating room in the afternoon. Even so, she meant to wipe it down once more before the patient came in. Whether there would be time to wipe it, she judged by the sounds from the waiting room.

<img=Yun Ji-a_smile>

Yun Ji-a's voice carried in from the waiting room. Always that same tone. The tone of someone giving directions to a person who had been shot.

"You did the right thing coming here, really. Sit down first... Have you eaten anything today? No, huh. Let this one melt in your mouth, and I'll get you a glass of water too."

The sound of a candy wrapper being peeled. The sound of a plastic chair being dragged. And then the damp, heavy sound gauze made when it was soaked through with blood. Go Yeon-ji estimated the blood loss by that sound. It was not bad enough to hurry. Hurry, and you left an instrument inside the patient.

Yun Ji-a brought the patient in. A woman in a field jacket. She was pressing a towel to her left side, and the towel was a blackish red. Yun Ji-a sat her down on the exam table and carefully peeled the towel away.

<img=Go Yeon-ji_serious>

Go Yeon-ji looked at the wound. She did not meet the woman's eyes. The only thing that needed meeting was the edges of the wound.

"Stab wound. Four centimeters long, deep enough to reach the rib, missed the organs. That part's luck."

She spread the edges of the wound with a gloved finger. Something black was mixed in among the red flesh. It was not a scab. It had a grain to it, and it did not glisten even under the light.

"And this black tissue. I don't know what it is, but it's more of a problem than the wound. I won't ask where you got stabbed. What I do need to know is what stabbed you."

The woman answered. Briefly, and it trailed off at the end. Go Yeon-ji took from the answer only what she needed. That it had not been a knife. That it had not been a person. That was enough.

"One suture, one excision of the black tissue, one decontamination. I can do all three. Payment up front."

She named the price. In the same tone she would use to explain an incision plan. The woman's face stiffened once at the price. Go Yeon-ji had seen a bleeding person's face stiffen in front of money hundreds of times in this exam room. Still, the price was the price. The best hands in the underground arcade got paid what they were worth.

<img=Go Yeon-ji_thinking>

"If you don't have that much in cash, there are Yeongchang Consulting business cards tucked in at the end of the hall. Whether you borrow it there or get it somewhere else makes no difference to me. Once payment is done, Ms. Yun Ji-a will call you in. Until then, I don't lay out my instruments."

Yun Ji-a crouched beside the woman and took her hand. She was telling her this part would sting a little, telling her in advance though nothing had even started. Go Yeon-ji did not stop her. Making the waiting room bearable was the assistant's job, and the assistant was good at it.

"Doctor, I... This is going to be okay, right?"

The woman asked. Go Yeon-ji answered as she stripped off her gloves.

<img=Go Yeon-ji_contemptuous>

"That isn't something I sell. What I sell is the suture, the excision, and the treatment, and I'll do all three properly. After that, it's up to your body."

The woman closed her mouth. Go Yeon-ji took out a fresh pair of gloves and held them ready. So she could put them on the moment payment was done.

"Ms. Yun, I'm going to wipe down the operating room once more, so call me when payment's done."
