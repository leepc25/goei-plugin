Gallery 3. The walls breathed.

Past the door built from long human bones joined end to end, everything inside was flesh. The floor, the walls, the ceiling. It was warm, and it swelled and settled in time with a heartbeat. The smell of raw meat and bile, and beneath it, the smell of something sweetly rotting.

Half-embedded in the middle of the wall was the material. The one brought over from Gallery 2 yesterday. It was sunk into the flesh wall up to the waist, both arms spread out to either side and fixed inside the wall. Its eyes were open. Covering the eyes is the last step.

<img=Aestheticism_serious>

Aestheticism took up a needle and began stitching from just below the material's left collarbone. The work was joining skin to the flesh of the wall. One stitch, then another. The material screamed. The other things buried in the wall screamed along with it. Aestheticism's hands did not stop.

"Hold still. It'll come out crooked."

At the twelfth stitch, the hands came away. Aestheticism stepped back two paces and looked. The line of the shoulders matched the grain of the wall. The title had been decided yesterday. *Arms Spread Wide, and Still Embracing No One*.

"It's coming along nicely. Just bear with it a little longer."

A sound came from the direction of the door. Not the bone door, but bare feet stepping onto the flesh floor and slipping.

<img=Aestheticism_curious>

Aestheticism left the needle stuck in the material's chest and turned around. High heels stepped on the flesh floor. *Ttogak. Ttogak.* A woman was standing just inside the door. A visitor who had come through the dark side of Gallery 1. She was clutching her head in both hands. The headache comes before the footsteps.

The woman looked at the material embedded in the wall. Then she looked at Aestheticism. Her mouth fell open.

"What is art to you?"

The woman backed away. Her back hit the bone door. The door did not open.

"...That person's alive. They're alive..."

"Mm. It has to be alive to be a work of art."

The woman turned and pounded on the door. Twice, three times. Aestheticism walked. Did not run. At that distance there was no need to run. The woman gave up on the door and ran along the wall. Her feet slid on the flesh floor, and one of the hands buried in the wall caught her by the ankle. The woman fell.

<img=Aestheticism_smile>

Aestheticism stood before her. Bent at the knees to meet her at eye level. The joints folded backward.

"Didn't get very far, did you?"

The woman tried to tear off the hand gripping her ankle. The hand would not let go. Aestheticism looked at the woman's face. It was a face stricken with fear. And yet earlier, when she had looked at the material in the wall, for just the briefest moment, she had not been able to look away. Aestheticism had seen that.

"You saw it, earlier. That. You couldn't take your eyes off it."

"N-no, I..."

"It's all right. Everyone does."

Aestheticism took the woman by the wrist and pulled her up. The mannequin hand was cold, and its strength was not the kind anyone could slip free of. They walked toward the wall. Beside the material, there was one empty space in the flesh wall. Just wide enough to spread two arms.

<img=Aestheticism_lovestruck>

"Don't worry. You're my finest work."
