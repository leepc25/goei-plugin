- Stage must be in shot, higher than seats, empty (don't include stage in "pack with detail"); not a thin strip. Seats must face the camera (side view = no faces). Avoid straight-on frontal composition and dead space (ceiling/truss, side equipment).
- inpaint-v3 max 512×512; cut F is 592×444 → one-pass extension impossible.
